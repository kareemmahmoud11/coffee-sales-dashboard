# coffee-sales-Analysis
Power BI Dashboard for Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

The workflow followed a **two-step approach**:  
1. **SQL Queries & Advanced Analysis** – to clean, transform, and explore raw transactional data, uncovering important insights such as top-selling products, peak sales hours, and weekday vs. weekend trends.  
2. **Power BI Dashboard** – to visualize these insights in an interactive format, enabling decision-makers to track KPIs, explore product categories, and monitor sales growth effectively.  

---

🔍 Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI Dashboard built for a new coffee and beverages brand launched in 2023. The dashboard analyzes sales data from the first 6 months of operation and includes key performance insights and category analysis.

🎯 Objective
To deliver actionable insights into sales trends, customer preferences, and product performance. By integrating **SQL-based analytics** and **Power BI visualizations**, the project provides a solid foundation for data-driven decision-making and helps set realistic KPIs for the second half of the year.  

---

📊 **Key Features**  
- 📊 **SQL Analysis (Before Dashboard)**  
  - Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation  
  - Advanced queries to analyze sales by product, category, hour, and day of the week  
  - Identification of high-performing products and categories  
  - Extraction of repeat purchase behavior and revenue contribution insights
---
- 📈 **Power BI Dashboard (After SQL Analysis)**  
  - Visual summary of monthly sales and customer behavior  
  - Breakdown of sales across top 10 product categories (coffee, tea, chocolate, baked goods, etc.)  
  - Interactive filters for flavor, location, and product type  
  - KPI cards highlighting total revenue, total orders, and average spend per customer  
  - Projection section to display target metrics for the upcoming 6 months  
  - Time Series Analysis: hourly, daily, and monthly trends to uncover growth trajectory and peak demand
